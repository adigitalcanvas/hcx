⚡ Excel efficiency: small improvements, big impact

With a few structural choices — named tables, consistent references, and summary formulas — you can turn a static spreadsheet into a living dashboard. This template uses:

  • Excel Tables (Table1, ReqOnSite) – so ranges expand automatically.
  • SUMIFS / COUNTIFS – for dynamic aggregations across projects, departments, and national categories.
  • GETPIVOTDATA – to pull clean summaries from pivot-style layouts.
  • Visual progress bars – using bar charts to show completion at a glance.
